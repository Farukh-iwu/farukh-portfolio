Artifact Title

AI Research Tools Evaluation: ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Perplexity

Introduction

AI research assistants differ widely in speed, depth, and presentation. Choosing the right one for a task needs evidence, not brand preference.

Description

A side-by-side evaluation of four AI research assistants on the same research task, scored across performance, output quality, and user experience criteria.

Objective

Identify the strengths of each tool and provide guidance for selecting AI assistants based on specific research requirements and workflow preferences.

Process

Ran the same deep-research prompt in each tool, recorded speed and behavior, compared outputs on 10 criteria in three groups, named a winner per criterion, and summarized key findings.

Tools and Tech Used

ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Perplexity research modes; HTML/CSS for the evaluation report; GitHub Pages for publishing.

Value Proposition of the Artifact

Gives teams a practical basis for picking an AI research tool by matching tool strengths to research depth, presentation needs, and time constraints.

Unique Value

Every result is backed by a shared link to the original research session, so reviewers can verify the evaluation themselves.

Relevance

Relevant for AI engineers, researchers, and managers selecting AI tooling for research and development workflows.

References

Shared research sessions from ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Perplexity (linked in Research Links below).

Research Overview

This evaluation analyzes four leading AI research assistants across critical performance categories. The systematic assessment provides valuable insights for selecting appropriate tools based on specific research requirements and workflow preferences.

Key Findings & Insights

🏆 Performance Leaders

DeepSeek and Gemini dominated speed and efficiency categories, completing tasks in 2.5 minutes compared to ChatGPT's 12 minutes.

🎯 Output Quality

DeepSeek excelled in precision and consistency, while Gemini provided the most user-friendly and presentable outputs.

🔍 Research Depth

Perplexity demonstrated superior research capabilities with 106 references, far exceeding other tools in citation depth and academic rigor.

💡 User Experience

Gemini offered the best interface design, while DeepSeek provided unique real-time process visibility during task execution.
